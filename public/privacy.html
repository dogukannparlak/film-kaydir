What we process, why, and on what legal basis

  • Data on your device: without an account your lists, seen titles, filters and keys live only in that tab (sessionStorage), are never sent to a server and are deleted when you close the tab. When signed in they are also kept in your browser (localStorage) and removed from the device when you sign out. Device settings such as language and privacy choice stay in your browser. Strictly necessary for the site to work; no cookies are used.
  • Account (optional): your e-mail address, a hash of your password (Supabase Auth), your lists, seen titles and filters, to sync across devices. Legal basis: performance of the service / contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), KVKK Art. 5/2-c).
  • Your API keys: by default only in your browser. If you turn it on in Settings they are stored encrypted in your account (the key is derived from your password in your browser; the plain keys never reach the server).
  • Statistics (only with your consent): visit count, time on site, page path, referring site name, device type, browser language; a random ID is stored in your browser to count return visits. Umami (cookie-free analytics) is loaded only with this consent too. Legal basis: consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), KVKK Art. 5/1).
  • Anonymous totals (even without consent): how many times the consent bar was shown and which first choices were made (accept / partial / reject), plus how many pages were loaded without consent; no identity, IP, page name or time-linked record is kept — only single-row aggregate counters. As this contains no personal data, it falls outside GDPR/KVKK scope and is unrelated to the erasure/portability rights that personal data triggers.
  • Activity log (only with your consent): what you do on the site: titles you swipe, searches, filters, views you open, buttons you click. To improve the site and debug errors. Without an account it is linked only to the random ID; when signed in, to your account. Passwords, e-mail fields and API key contents are never recorded. Legal basis: consent.
  • Error reports: if something breaks, the error message and location are sent with a temporary ID that is not stored on your device (with your account if signed in), to keep the site working. Legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), KVKK Art. 5/2-f).
  • Server and security logs: the hosting (Cloudflare) and database (Supabase) providers briefly keep IP addresses and request data to prevent abuse (rate limiting) and for security. Legal basis: legitimate interest.

You don't have to consent to statistics or the activity log; the site works the same without them. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings › Privacy and your data; withdrawal does not affect processing before it.

Recipients and international transfers

  • Supabase (database, accounts): data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt).
  • Cloudflare (hosting, TMDB proxy, aggregate page counts via Web Analytics): US-based, servers worldwide.
  • Umami Cloud (only with Statistics consent): cookie-free aggregate visit counts.
  • TMDB, OMDb, YouTube: for posters, scores and trailers your browser requests these services directly (they see your IP address).

Some of these services are outside your country; your consent to the optional processing also covers the transfers it requires. Data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Retention

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Activity log and error reports: 90 days.
  • Visit statistics: 13 months.
  • Anonymous aggregate counters (consent bar shown/choices, non-consented loads): indefinite; they contain no personal data.
  • Data without an account: until you close the tab. Device copy while signed in: until you sign out.

Your rights

Under GDPR Art. 15–22 and KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to object, to withdraw consent and to data portability. Most of them you can use yourself, right away:

  • Download my data: Settings › Privacy and your data (account, lists, activity and visit records as JSON).
  • Delete my account and data: same section; your account and all server-side records are deleted immediately.
  • Delete this device's records: if you used the site without an account, deletes the visit and activity records of this device.

For anything else write to dogukannparlak@gmail.com; you'll get an answer within 30 days. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Turkey: the KVKK Board, kvkk.gov.tr).

What the site owner sees

On the admin page the site owner sees aggregate visit counts, each account's e-mail address, sign-up and last sign-in dates, the account's lists (Want, Watched, Passed; for support and debugging) and, if you consented, your activity log. Visitors who decline consent are counted only in anonymous totals (bar shown and choice counts, non-consented loads); who they are, or even which pages they visited, is not recorded. The owner cannot see your API keys or password.
