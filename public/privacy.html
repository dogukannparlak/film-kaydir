Privacy

Kaydır · last updated October 19, 2026

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, if you sign in.
  • Your lists: the movies and shows you add to your Want, Watched and Pass lists, your marks, your filters and the titles you have seen.
  • Your API keys: your TMDB and OMDb keys stay only in your browser by default. If you turn on "Store my API keys in my account" in Settings, they are stored encrypted in your account: the encryption key is derived in your browser from your account password (PBKDF2 + AES-GCM), and neither the plain keys nor this encryption key are sent to the server. If you reset your password, the old copy can no longer be opened and you enter your keys again.
  • Anonymous visit statistics: how many people visit and how long they stay; the page path, the name of the site you came from, device type (phone/computer), browser language and whether you are signed in. No cookies are used and IP addresses are not written to the statistics. So that return visits can be counted, a random ID is stored in your browser (localStorage); it contains no personal information and does not identify you. Clearing your browser data removes it. In addition, cookie-free Umami and Cloudflare Web Analytics keep aggregate visit counts.

If you don't create an account, your lists never leave your device; the data stays only in your browser (localStorage). The only thing sent to a server is the anonymous statistics above.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored on Supabase (EU, Frankfurt). Only its owner can access each row; other users cannot see your data. On the admin page the site owner sees aggregate visit counts and each account's e-mail address, sign-up and last sign-in dates and number of list items; not the content of your lists or your API keys. Account deletion requests are handled there.

Who it is shared with

It is never sold or shared with anyone. Your browser sends requests directly to TMDB for movie information and posters, to OMDb for ratings and to YouTube for trailers; these requests are made with your own key (or through the site's shared TMDB connection).

Deletion

To delete your account and all of your data, write to dogukannparlak@gmail.com. You can delete the data in your browser from Settings or by clearing your browser data.
